- Medium used for telecom between network devices
- Wired and wireless
1. **Bounded Media** (Physical)
   - Copper
    - Twisted Pair
    - Color coded wrapped in plastic
    - Pairs wrapped in another plastic sheath
    - Uses physical conductor like metal wire or glass or plastic core cable
   -  Coax
   - Fiber Optic
2. Unbounded Media (Wireless)
   - Radio Networking
   - Spread Spectrum
     1. Direct Sequence Spread Spectrum (DSSS)
     2. Frequency Hopping Spread Spectrum (FHSS)
   - Infrared
   - Bluetooth
   - Microwave
   - Wireless Access Point
