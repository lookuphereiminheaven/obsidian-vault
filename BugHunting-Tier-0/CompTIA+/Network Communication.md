- Data Transmission
1. Instantaneous
2. Serial
    - Transmission of bits once per clock cycle (CPU)
      - 1 byte = 8 serial bits
      - Synchronous: Use time intervals and must be in sync
      - Asynchronous: Special start and stop patterns between each byte
3. Parallel
   - Multiple transmission lines, wires, cables simultaneously
   - 1 character is 8 parallel bits
   - Used in SCSI, Parallel ports and PC Card bus
4. Baseband
   - Digital signals sent via DC pulses
   - All devices share the medium and cant transmit and receive simultaneously
   - Multiple baseband channels can be combined over a medium using a channel with specific time slots
